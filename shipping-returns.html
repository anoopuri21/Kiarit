Shipping & Returns

Clear timelines, honest charges and a straightforward replacement process — everything you need to know before you order.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹999.
  • Flat ₹79 shipping on orders below ₹999.
  • Cash on delivery is available on selected PIN codes and carries an additional handling fee, confirmed at the time of order.

Dispatch Timeline

Orders confirmed and paid before 4:00 PM on a working day are usually dispatched the same day. All other orders are dispatched within 24 working hours. We do not dispatch on Sundays or public holidays.

You will receive the courier name and tracking number on WhatsApp as soon as your parcel leaves our facility.

Delivery Timeline

  • Metro cities — typically 3 to 5 business days from dispatch.
  • Other cities and towns — typically 5 to 7 business days from dispatch.
  • Remote and hill regions — may take 7 to 10 business days.

These are courier estimates, not guarantees. Festive periods, weather disruptions and regional restrictions can extend delivery times.

Order Tracking

Send your order number on WhatsApp at +91 85878 75749 and we will share the live status. If tracking has not updated for more than 72 hours, tell us and we will raise a query with the courier on your behalf.

Damaged or Incorrect Parcels

We pack every order in tamper-evident packaging, but transit accidents happen. If your parcel arrives damaged, leaking or containing the wrong item:

  • Report it within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Send us a photograph of the outer packaging, the shipping label and the affected product. An unboxing video is ideal but not mandatory.
  • Once verified, we ship a free replacement at our cost. You do not pay return shipping.
  • Where a replacement is unavailable, we issue a full refund instead.

Returns of Sealed Goods

For hygiene and safety reasons, cosmetic, dermatological and nutraceutical products cannot be returned once the seal is broken. This is standard practice for personal-care goods in India and protects every customer.

Unopened products with an intact seal may be returned within 7 days of delivery if you have changed your mind. The product must be in its original packaging and in resaleable condition. Return shipping in this case is borne by the customer, and the refund covers the product value less the original shipping cost.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Any product whose seal, shrink wrap or safety cap has been opened.
  • Products returned without their original packaging, outer carton or batch label.
  • Items reported more than 7 days after delivery, except in the case of transit damage reported within 48 hours.
  • Products damaged by misuse, incorrect storage or use beyond the printed expiry date.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the returned item reaching us, or of a damage claim being approved. Your bank may take a further 2 to 3 days to reflect the credit. We share the refund reference number with you as soon as it is issued.

Order Cancellation

You may cancel a confirmed order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched — simply message us. Once the parcel has left our facility it cannot be cancelled, but you may refuse delivery, after which the standard refund process applies less the shipping cost actually incurred.

Undelivered Parcels

If a courier is unable to deliver after three attempts, or if the address is incorrect or incomplete, the parcel returns to us. We will contact you to arrange redelivery, for which the shipping charge applies again, or to process a refund of the product value.

Questions about this policy? Write to us at kiaritpharma@gmail.com or call +91 85878 75749. We respond within one working day.
